OUR MINISTERS



GALON JONES
PULPIT MINISTER

Galon Jones grew up in the north Dallas area. He has been married to Sharon for over forty years and has three children and five grandchildren. He has a BS in Theology from Harding University. He has master's degrees in Theology, Marriage and Family Therapy, Conflict Resolution, Mediation, Negotiation and Divorce Mediation from Abilene Christian University. He served over 10 years church planting in Florianopolis, Brasil.

Before coming to Webb Chapel, he served on staff for twenty years, in various ministry positions at the Greenville Oaks Church in Allen, TX. He currently serves as director of James Group Ministries.



DAVID BATES
OUTREACH AND INVOLVMENT MINISTER

David Bates has attended Webb Chapel since 1996, where he's been a teacher and worship-leader for many years. After many years of working in creative management, David was installed as a co-Minister, along with Galon Jones, in October of 2023. He also serves as Board President of James Group Ministries, a faith-based recovery program.

David completed his Bachelor of Arts at Oklahoma Christian in 1990 and is working toward a Master of Fine Arts in Visual Communication at Texas A&M Commerce-Dallas. He and his wife Gaymarie live in Farmers Branch, Texas.



HARMON HALE
YOUTH MINISTER

Harmon Hale joined Webb Chapel In August of 2022. Harmon grew up in Lewisville, Texas where he met his wife Charisma. The two were married on July 12, 2021. Harmon completed his Bachelor of Science degree in Bible with emphasis in Youth and Family Ministry and Apologetics. He graduated from Freed-Hardeman University in 2020.
