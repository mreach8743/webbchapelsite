CURRENT QUARTER BIBLE CLASSES

DUE TO THE CURRENT COVID-19 PANDEMIC, WE ARE NOT CONDUCTING IN-PERSON CLASSES. Please be in prayer that this pandemic is contained and that we may return to normal activities soon.

To support those desiring to be more consistent in their Bible reading, we are meeting on Zoom on Wednesday mornings or Wednesday evenings to discuss the assigned reading. The assignments each week are intended to be manageable. Many of them can be read in a day or two, but the recommended approach is to spend a few minutes each day reading and then review your reading and make some notes about what stood out to you - something God revealed about himself, something that is a challenge to you or encouraging.
