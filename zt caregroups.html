CARE GROUPS



Group Ministry at Webb Chapel is dedicated to meeting the needs of the Webb Chapel members through the strengthening of our relationships with one another and by providing help in times of need. Everyone at Webb Chapel is part of one of the 9 Care Groups. Each group meets together sometime during the fourth weekend of each month.They were missionaries in Adelaide, Australia for 9 years, leaving an established Church when they moved back to the United States. Herman taught at the Preston Road School of Preaching later becoming the Preston Road School of Higher Education, from 1981 to 1995. Herman then taught at the South Pacific Bible College in New Zealand from 2006 to 2013. In 2013 he began working with Nations University serving as Vice Chancellor and Professor. He teaches, via skype, two days a week at the Cambodian Bible School and one day a week at Adelaide Church of Christ, via skype. Herman has also been preaching every Sunday at different churches. He and Rosanne currently reside in Irving, Texas. We began our financial support for them in 2015.
