GIVING METHODS

ONLINE

Website giving provides a fast, convenient and secure way for you to give one-time gifts or recurring donations by debit card, check card, or credit card. Your gift can also be drawn from your checking account or a savings account. Gifts can be designated for Contribution or Assistance.

MOBILE APP

Using the "Realm-Connect" Application from your phone you can give one-time gifts or recurring donations by debit card, check card, or credit card. Your gift can also be drawn from your checking account or a savings account. Gifts can be designated for Contribution or Assistance.


TEXT MESSAGE

Text the word WCCCGIVE to 73256. Your one-time gift can be made by debit card, check card, or credit card. Your gift can also be drawn from your checking account or a savings account. Gifts can be designated for Contribution or Assistance.



BILL PAY

You can also set up Webb Chapel in your banking Bill-Pay service. When using this method, please use this mailing address: Webb Chapel Church of Christ, 13425 Webb Chapel Rd., Dallas, Texas 75234, and note if the gift is for the Contribution or Assistance.

MATTHEW 25:21

His master said to him, "Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a little: I will set you over much. Enter into the joy of your master."

Like the servants in the Parable of the Talents, we will be required to give an account of what we have done with everything we have been blessed with. This means our time, our money, and our talents. It is all God’s. (Psalm 24:1) And we will give an accounting to the owner who has entrusted all things to us.
